Tierscope · tierscope-app.vercel.app

Find the revenue your pricing leaves on the table.

Tierscope is a free pricing check for SaaS teams. Paste your pricing page and get a pricing score out of 100 in under a minute. Enter a work email to unlock the full report: your plans compared with your 20 closest competitors, three recommended changes and your estimated revenue gap per year.

The problem

Most SaaS teams have no pricing department

Prices are set once at launch, often copied from a competitor, and rarely revisited. Teams guess, and every month of the wrong price is revenue lost.

What we built

A lead magnet, an email engine and a CRM

  • A website that scores any pricing page for free
  • A full report, unlocked with a work email
  • Email 1 written by AI, plus one follow-up the next day
  • A private CRM that ranks every lead
What we want from a lead

A booked pricing review

A paid 60-minute session with a pricing strategist, who turns the report into a concrete plan.

Our best lead: the founder of a B2B SaaS with three paid plans who scores under 60.

Speaker notes: One sentence on the product, one on the problem, one on the business goal. The free check is the hook; the pricing review is what we sell.

Who it is for

Three people, one missing pricing expert

Persona 1 · The founder

Clara, co-founder & CEO

B2B SaaS, 25 people, three paid plans, raising a Series A in six months.

"Should we raise prices before our next round?"

Pain: no one owns pricing; she fears churn if prices go up, and investors keep asking about revenue per customer.

How Tierscope helps: a score and a revenue gap per year give her a number to act on; the report shows where each plan sits against 20 competitors and ranks three changes by revenue impact.

Next step: books the pricing review. She is our ideal lead.

Persona 2 · The product manager

Hugo, product manager

Mid-size SaaS, shipping a new feature (for example SSO or an audit log) next quarter.

"Which plan should this feature go in?"

Pain: packaging decided by gut feeling; features given away in a low plan that competitors charge for.

How Tierscope helps: the "Feature gaps" part of the report shows what he gives away that others charge for, and which plan competitors put each feature in.

Next step: shares the report with his team, then brings it to a review.

Persona 3 · The sales lead

Sofia, head of sales

B2B SaaS sold to mid-market companies, many deals lost on price.

"Why are you more expensive?"

Pain: no data to answer prospects, so the team discounts to close.

How Tierscope helps: the "Price position" part shows each plan against the market middle and the cheapest rival; "Discounts & trials" shows how their offers compare with the norm.

Next step: uses the report as talking points, and asks for a review to fix the plans behind the objection.

Personas are illustrative. A lead we ignore: someone trying the demo links (linear.app, hubspot.com) with a personal email.

Speaker notes: Each persona has one question Tierscope answers in under a minute. Clara is who we rank highest in the CRM.

How it works

One visit, two emails, zero manual work

1 · PasteThe visitor pastes their pricing page URL.
2 · ScoreA free pricing score out of 100, printed like a receipt.
3 · UnlockWork email, consent box, one-time code.
4 · Report20 competitors, three changes, the revenue gap.
5 · Email 1Seconds later, written by Gemini, sent from our Gmail.
6 · Follow-upOne email the next day, in the same thread.
Email 1, written by AI

Gemini writes a short, personal email from the visitor's answers: their company, score, revenue gap and category. It offers one next step: reply to book a 60-minute pricing review.

We tested the prompt on three leads (good, vague and a trap with fake instructions) over three versions. If Gemini is busy, a fixed text is sent instead.

Rules we chose
  • Emails only to people who tick the consent box
  • Every unlock sends a new email 1, and each email 1 gets its own follow-up
  • One follow-up only, sent by a daily robot at 9:00 (Paris)
  • A lead marked "lost" (for example after "stop") is never emailed again
Safe by design
  • Limits: 5 emails a day per address, 30 an hour, 100 a day
  • What visitors type is cleaned before it reaches an email: no injected text, links or discounts
  • Keys stay on the server; the browser never talks to the database or Gmail
  • Runs in the cloud: no laptop needed

Built with: a website and server routes on Vercel · Supabase database · Gmail (sending through an app password) · Google Gemini · a daily Vercel Cron job.

Speaker notes: The visitor gets value first (the score), then gives an email for more. Everything after that is automatic, and protected against abuse of our Gmail.

The CRM · /admin

Every lead, ranked, with the next step one click away

Overview

A private page behind a password, checked on the server.

  • Leads, hot leads, consent rate, average score
  • Open opportunity: the revenue gaps of all open leads added up
  • Reply rate, meetings, won
  • Funnel (all leads → contacted → replied → meeting → won) and new leads per day
Ranking from 0 to 100

Hot 70+ · Warm 45–69 · Cold under 45 · Ignore: tried a demo link. Each lead shows the reasons behind its score.

Working the leads
  • Status: new → contacted → followed up → replied → meeting → won or lost
  • Send email next to each status: Gmail sends email 1 at once, no mail app
  • Run robot now and Send follow-up now for tests
  • Notes, filters (priority, status, category, consent), CSV export
  • Every email sent to a lead is listed on that lead
  • A "Sample data" switch with fake leads, for demos

Daily routine: open Leads sorted by priority, answer the hot ones, mark replies and meetings, mark "lost" anyone who asks to stop.

Speaker notes: The CRM tells the team who to call first and why. Close on the goal: more booked pricing reviews with less manual work.
