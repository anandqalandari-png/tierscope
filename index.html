Questions

Why do you need my email?+

Your score is free and instant. The full report is sent to your inbox so you can keep and share it, and we use the same address to alert you when competitors change prices. You can unsubscribe in one click.

Where does the competitor data come from?+

From competitors' public pricing pages and plan comparison tables. Every number in your report links to the page it came from, captured on the day the report was made.

Do I need to share revenue or customer data?+

No. The report only uses your public pricing page. In a paid review you can choose to share your plan mix so we can size the revenue impact more precisely.

How accurate is the competitor matching?+

The report lists every competitor it used and how closely each one matches. You can remove any that don't fit or add ones it missed.
